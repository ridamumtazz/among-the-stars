    How the museum works

    Three steps, no hurry. The machines have waited this long.

    1. Pick a world

      Open the Moon or Mars globe, or cross to the spacecraft beyond.

    2. Click a glowing dot

      Each dot is a real machine at a place recorded in NASA's records.

    3. Read its story and test what you learned

      Open the exhibit to see what the machine did and where it is now.
